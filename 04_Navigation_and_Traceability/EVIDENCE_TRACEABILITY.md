# Evidence traceability

Chain: Research question → experiment design → benchmark implementation → raw measurements → statistical analysis → result/interpretation → evidence file.
All paths are relative to `02_PerfLens_Project/`. "E-labels" are navigation labels invented for this package; the authoritative identifiers are the timestamped folder names. Where an archive does not document a field, it says *Not documented* / *Not available*. "Runner snapshot" = frozen copy of `run.py`, `benchmark.py`, `analyze.py`, `perflens.py`, … stored in the run's own `source/` folder.

Statistical vocabulary used throughout: *statistical direction* (interval excludes 0) ≠ *practical decision* (interval relative to ±5 %). Observations are fresh processes or JMH forks, never inner iterations; intervals are marginal percentile bootstrap, 5 000 resamples of whole pairs; no multiple-comparison correction.
